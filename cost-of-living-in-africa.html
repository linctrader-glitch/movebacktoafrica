Read before you plan

Rent in Nigeria is quoted per year; figures here are the monthly share. Lagos adds agency, legal and service charges in year one.

School fees are annual tuition divided by 12, before registration and building levies that can reach $10,000 at top capital schools. Rubavu, Saly and Grand-Bassam have no full international school; families usually commute to or board in the capital.

Calm city prices rest on thinner data, so their ranges run wider on purpose. Health insurance and some eating out and household help figures are built up from national prices. Planning ranges, not quotes.
