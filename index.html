Why people's moves back home go wrong

I've watched too many people move home on emotion and lose their savings, their peace, or both. These are the three problems I hear about most.

No income plan

Savings run out faster than anyone expects. Without income that keeps coming, many returnees end up moving abroad again.

Trusting the wrong people

The biggest losses rarely come from strangers. They come from relatives, caretakers, hired professionals and even other returnees.

The first 30 days

Power cuts, rising rents, slow paperwork and feeling like an outsider. Social media makes moving back look easier than it is.

Treat your move like a business project

Plan it, budget it, test it, and then do it. That is how I build businesses in Nigeria from abroad, and it is how you should plan your move.
